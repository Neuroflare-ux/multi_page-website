Intranet

What is an Intranet?

An intranet is a private network used within an organisation. It uses the same technology as the internet but is only accessible to employees or members of that organisation.

What is it Used For?

Intranets are used for sharing internal documents, company news, HR information, and internal communication tools like messaging or email.

Intranet vs Internet

The internet is open to everyone. An intranet is private and restricted to authorised users inside an organisation.
